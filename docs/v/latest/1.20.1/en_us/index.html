                                                                                          Unravel

                                                                                          Media is another form of matter, therefore you can convert solid matter to media matter. This matter typically becomes strings, though i was unable to make any use of it, but a fellow caster - Willow had pointed out that they need to have a reference point, like a plant has a root. All media has a reference point and is kept together by it.


                                                                                          Media is not static, it is an ambient energy. For changing matter i have coined the following terms, to unravel() is to convert to media and to weave() is to convert it into solid matter. I have devised the following patterns to turn items into abstract energy and vice-versa.


                                                                                          Mediafy (entity → item)

                                                                                            • Palettes
                                                                                              Your browser does not support visualizing patterns. Pattern code: waaqaeq

                                                                                              Takes a item entity and weaves it into local media, then returns the mediafied item.
